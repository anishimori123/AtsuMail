AtsuMail Voice Lab experiment

Reads Japanese with the open-source yomiscript engine (Kokoro voice), inside your browser. First use downloads about 190 MB (use Wi-Fi). Japanese text only.
Paste Japanese text above, then press Read aloud.

Engine: receptron/yomiscript (Apache-2.0), Kokoro-82M voice (Apache-2.0), OpenJTalk dictionary. Readings here come from the dictionary, not from an AI, so some words are misread.
