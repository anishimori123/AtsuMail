AtsuMail - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

AtsuMail is a personal email and spreadsheet reader made by its owner for his own use. It runs entirely in your web browser. There is no AtsuMail server.

What data AtsuMail uses

  • Gmail (read-only): to show your folders (labels) and emails on screen.
  • Google Sheets (read-only): to show spreadsheets you choose in the settings.

AtsuMail cannot send, delete or change your emails or spreadsheets.

Where your data goes

  • Your data goes directly from Google to your browser. It is not sent to, stored on, or shared with any other server or person.
  • AtsuMail does not sell, share or transfer your data, and does not use it for ads.
  • AtsuMail only saves small display settings (text size, reading mode, voice choice) in your own browser.
  • The sign-in permission from Google lasts about 1 hour and is kept only in the open browser tab.

Read aloud

The read-aloud feature uses the voices built into your browser or device. Your browser or device maker may process that text to produce speech, under their own privacy policy.

Google API Services

AtsuMail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can remove AtsuMail's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/anishimori123/AtsuMail.
